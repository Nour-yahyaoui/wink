# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as creators, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **W.I.N.K** and our community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, gender identity, disability, ethnicity, or religion.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward fellow students and contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical and product feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the **W.I.N.K** community and user safety

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information (or test/student data) without explicit permission
- Intentionally introducing insecure code, backdoors, or malicious dependencies
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers (`@iyedomri` and `@Nour-yahyaoui`) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
